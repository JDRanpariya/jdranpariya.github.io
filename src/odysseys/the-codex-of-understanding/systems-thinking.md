## Resources

### Classics

- _Thinking in Systems_ by Donella Meadows
- _The Fifth Discipline_ by Peter Senge
- _General System Theory_ by Ludwig von Bertalanffy
- _Aristotle’s First Principles_
- René Descartes: _Discourse on Method_
- _Feynman’s Lecture: “Cargo Cult Science”_: thinking skeptically from first principles
- **Richard Feynman**: "Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!" (Problem-solving approach)
- **Charlie Munger**: "Poor Charlie's Almanack" (Mental models approach)
- **Norbert Wiener**: "Cybernetics" (1948)
- **Gregory Bateson**: "Steps to an Ecology of Mind" (1972)
- **Heinz von Foerster**: "Understanding Understanding" (2003)
- **Jay Forrester**: "Industrial Dynamics" (1961)
- **Russell Ackoff**: "Creating the Corporate Future" (1981)
- Systems Thinking Made Simple: New Hope for Solving Wicked Problems by Derek Cabrera
- The Systems Bible by John Gall
- Principles: Life and Work by Ray Dalio
- Zero to One: Notes on Startups by Peter Thiel
- **The Structure of Scientific Revolutions** - Thomas Kuhn
- **Self-Organization in Biological Systems** - Camazine et al.

### Modern

- https://systemsthinkingalliance.org/glossary/
- https://modelthinkers.com/playbook/starting-your-side-hustle
- [System Dynamics MIT Workshop](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o-Yp8A7BPE8&list=TLPQMTYxMDIwMjNIiV50pLYRNw&index=2)
- **Complexity: A Guided Tour** - Melanie Mitchell
- _Complex Adaptive Systems_ (Miller & Page)
- _Introduction to Systems Biology_ (Alon): feedback motifs in gene regulation.
- **Designing Regenerative Cultures** - Daniel Christian Wahl
- **Being You** - Anil Seth
- **The Embodied Mind** - Varela, Thompson, Rosch
