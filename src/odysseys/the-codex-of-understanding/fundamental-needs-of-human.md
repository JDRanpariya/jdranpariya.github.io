## Approach

### Fundamental and Early Developments

1. **Biological & Evolutionary Perspective**

    - **Focus**: Physiological and genetic basis of needs. How human needs have been shaped by evolutionary processes.
    - **Key Concepts**: Survival, reproduction, genetic fitness, Homeostasis, neurobiology, genetics.
    - **Contributors**: Charles Darwin, evolutionary psychologists like Leda Cosmides and John Tooby.
    - **Development**: Basic physiological needs for survival (food, water, shelter) are primary and must be met for any other needs to be considered. Fundamental needs for survival and reproduction drive behavior and social structures.
    - **Example**: The need for social bonds can be understood as an evolutionary adaptation to enhance survival and reproductive success through cooperation and support.
    - **Resources**
	    - **"The Selfish Gene" by Richard Dawkins**
	    - **"Evolutionary Psychology: The New Science of the Mind" by David Buss**
	    - The Moral Animal
	    - **"Evolutionary Psychology: A Primer" by Leda Cosmides and John Tooby**
	    - "Why Evolution is True" by Jerry Coyne
	    - "The Greatest Show on Earth" and "The Blind Watchmaker". by Richard Dawkins.
	    - "Evolution" by Mark Ridley.
	    - Edward O. Wilson with "The Social Conquest of Earth"
	    - [Evolutionary Biology](https://www.coursera.org/learn/evolutionary-biology)
	    - [Evolutionary Psychology](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL848F2368C90DDC3D)
	    - [Intro to Evolution](https://evolution.berkeley.edu/evolution-101/)
	    - [Stated Casually](https://www.youtube.com/@StatedCasually/videos)

### Parallel Development of Individual and Social Needs

3. **Social Group Living**

    - **Focus**: Formation of social groups and cooperative living.
    - **Key Concepts**: Social bonds, cooperation, safety in numbers.
    - **Development**: Social living provides safety, increases chances of survival and reproductive success, leading to complex social behaviors and structures.

5. **Cognitive Perspective**

    - **Focus**: Internal mental processes and problem-solving.
    - **Key Concepts**: Cognitive development, schema, self-efficacy.
    - **Development**: Cognitive abilities develop in parallel with social living, aiding in navigation of social environments and problem-solving.
6. **Psychoanalytic Perspective**

    - **Focus**: Unconscious motivations and early childhood experiences.
    - **Key Concepts**: Instinctual drives, stages of development.
    - **Development**: Psychological development influenced by early relationships and social structures.
7. **Humanistic Perspective**

    - **Focus**: Personal growth and self-actualization.
    - **Key Concepts**: Hierarchy of needs, self-actualization, unconditional positive regard.
    - **Development**: Emerges from a combination of individual psychological needs and social influences.
    - Resources: **"Toward a Psychology of Being" by Abraham Maslow**, **"On Becoming a Person: A Therapist's View of Psychotherapy" by Carl Rogers** , **"The Farther Reaches of Human Nature" by Abraham Maslow**,

### Economic and Environmental Interactions

8. **Economic Perspective**

    - **Focus**: Resource allocation and material well-being.
    - **Key Concepts**: Basic needs, utility, welfare economics.
    - **Development**: Economic systems develop as humans organize resources to meet both individual and social needs.
9. **Ecological Perspective**

    - **Focus**: Interaction between individuals and their environment.
    - **Key Concepts**: Ecosystem, sustainable development, environmental psychology.
    - **Development**: Human needs are influenced by and impact the environment, leading to sustainable practices and environmental awareness.

### Ethical, Political, and Technological Developments

10. **Philosophical Perspective**

    - **Focus**: Ethical and existential considerations of human needs.
    - **Key Concepts**: Eudaimonia, virtue ethics, existential needs.
    - **Development**: Reflects on the broader meaning and purpose of human life, influenced by earlier developments in psychology, society, and culture.
11. **Political Perspective**

    - **Focus**: Influence of power, governance, and policy on human needs.
    - **Key Concepts**: Human rights, social justice, welfare state.
    - **Development**: Political systems evolve to manage social structures and address collective needs, influenced by economic and sociocultural factors.
12. **Technological Perspective**

    - **Focus**: Impact of technology on meeting human needs.
    - **Key Concepts**: Digital divide, technological determinism, human-computer interaction.
    - **Development**: Technology evolves alongside human needs, transforming how needs are met and creating new needs in the process.
