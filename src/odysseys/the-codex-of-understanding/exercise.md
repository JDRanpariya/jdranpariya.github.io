## Beliefs
- Training 3-4 times a week is enough, better to take rest of 48 hours before training the same muscles.
- Don't do circuits, one exercise 2-3 sets and 4-8 sets per muscle group per session.
- Stick to same exercise for 2-3 months, keep adding more reps,weight or difficulty every week.
- Rest 1-3 min between sets for hypertrophy

- It helps with cognitive function.
- Need to learn more.
