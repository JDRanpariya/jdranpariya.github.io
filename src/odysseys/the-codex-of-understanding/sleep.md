## Beliefs
- Sleep is most foundational to my health and never get less then 7-8 hours of it.
- Control your waking up to control bed time.
- It's relatively easier to wake up when you're tired then going to bed when you're not.
- Use fatigue throughout the day to create pressure.
- High fat diet increases Orexin and high carb diet reduces it, so carbs would help sleep faster.
- Any kind of work that my brain knows I haven't done for today, will keep me awake

## Habits
- Get out of bed as soon as you wakeup
- Expose yourself to light and exercise as soon as you wakeup
- Keep morning hours productive
- Eat early, 3 hours before bedtime.
- Limit fats to 20 in evening meals
- Remove access to device after 8, very strictly
- Give space, unstructured time for brain to process emotions and stress
- Have consistent bed times. Try to be on time and not 15m early or late.

## Routine & Environment

- Sleep shall range between 7-9
- Start winding up around 8, 11 is hard limit for light to my eyes.
- Have night lights or lights around in night below on ground and not overhead. Better have it dim. Candle light and fireplaces are fine.
- Keep your lights low in the night.
- Sleep before 9.45
- No screen time after 8 PM

## Sleep & Wakefulness

- What determines our sleep and quality of wakeful state
	- Adenosine -> Chemical force
		- builds up while we are awake
		- creates sleep hunger
	- Circadian Force
		- Cortisol is released from adrenals in morning
		- It's better to have it early in the day
		- A cellular timer is set to release Melatonin from pineal gland after 12-14 hours
		- pineal Gland -> only organ to release Melatonin 
		- Retinal Ganglion cells perceives particular type of light and signal is sent to suprachiasmatic nucleus -> It has connection to every cell of body.
		- This Neurons don't know what's sunlight but they respond best to sunlight that we get when sun is low in the sky.
		- **Get Sunlight early in the morning 2-10 minutes, preferably sun rise, or get it from artificial light blue, yellow light.**
		- **Get outside sometime around sun set**
		- **Viewing sunlight through glass is half as effective**.
		- Timing of taking food, timing of exercise or intake of drugs also helps. But Light is direct input and main way.
		- inter-geniculate leaflet -> governs non-photonic phenomena like exercise and food.
		- From 11 to 4 in the night, Light from eyes to the retina to the structure called Habenula(Disappointment Nucleus) -> looks like two little bat ears sitting right in the middle of thalamus. Suppresses the release of dopamine.
	- Wakeup earlier
