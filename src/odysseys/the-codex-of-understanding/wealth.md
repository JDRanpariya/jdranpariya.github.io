### 🧭 Strategic Pathway

We'll work in **3 parallel tracks**, which synergize:

|Track|Focus|Purpose|

#### 🧠 Track A: Quant + Algo (Your Technical Arena)

You're ideal for this because you already do AI. You can beat traditional finance people who don't understand modern ML.

##### 🔧 Steps

1. **Month 0–1: Foundations**
    - Learn quantitative finance basics:
        - Book: _Quantitative Trading_ – Ernest Chan
        - Course: [QuantInsti EPAT YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@QuantInsti)
    - Simulate basic strategies on:
        - **QuantConnect** or **Backtrader** (Python)
2. **Month 2–3: Build Micro-Algos**
    - Build small, rule-based strategies:
        - Mean reversion
        - Momentum
        - Volatility breakout
    - Use historical data and backtest on your laptop or Google Colab
3. **Month 4+: AI Edge**
    - Integrate:
        - Reinforcement learning (e.g., bandits for order sizing)
        - Deep learning for sentiment/trend analysis
    - Use **JAX**, your AI stack, for speed
    - Explore competition platforms: **Numerai**, **Kaggle** (finance contests)

##### 🧰 Tools

- Data: Yahoo Finance (free), Tiingo (cheap), Quandl (academic access)
- Infra: Google Colab, Jupyter, JAX, QuantConnect
- Paper trading: Alpaca, TradingView
