## Core Focus Areas

- Systems Thinking
- Human-Centered Design
- Failure and Resilience
- Engineering Fundamentals
- Nature & Biomimicry
- Personal Development Mindset

| Month | Book | Why? |
| ----- | ---- | ---- |
| 1 | **The Design of Everyday Things** – Don Norman | Foundation of design. Teaches how people interact with systems. |
| 1 | **How to Fail at Almost Everything and Still Win Big** – Scott Adams | Cultivates resilience and anti-fragility. |
| 2 | **Thinking in Systems** – Donella Meadows | Master feedback loops, leverage points, system failure. |
| 3 | **Success Through Failure** – Henry Petroski | Learn how innovation arises from error. |
| 4 | **Structures: Or Why Things Don't Fall Down** – J.E. Gordon | Grounding in physical forces & mechanical intuition. |
| 5 | **The Gecko's Foot** – Peter Forbes | Nature-inspired engineering: the real edge. |
| 6 | **Designing Regenerative Cultures** – Daniel Wahl | Systems + sustainability + culture as unified practice. |
| 7 | **Sustainable Materials – With Both Eyes Open** | Understand the materials bottlenecks of your utopia. |
| 8 | **Engineer to Win** – Carroll Smith | Mindset for high performance and real-world systems. |
| 9 | **Human Compatible** – Stuart Russell | AI alignment, ethics: the most serious risks. |
| 10 | **The Systems Bible** – John Gall | How complex systems fail; you'll laugh and learn. |
| 11 | **An Astronaut's Guide to Life** – Chris Hadfield | Discipline, humility, and awe (from an edge profession). |
| 12 | **The Future We Choose** – Figueres & Rivett-Carnac | Systems-level, climate-focused vision for action. |
| 13 | **The Selfish Gene** – Richard Dawkins | Understand how complex systems emerge and evolve. |
| 14 | **The Beginning of Infinity** – David Deutsch | Deep thinking on progress, science, and optimism. |
| 15 | **Architects of Intelligence** – Martin Ford | Survey of the future of AI from the experts themselves. |
| 16 | **How Do Wings Work?** – Holger Babinsky | Advanced, focused study of physics and engineering myths. |
| 17–18 | **Your Own Exploration** – Start diving into research papers, e.g., from arXiv, Nature AI, or MIT Media Lab | Practice evaluating new ideas critically & systemically. |
