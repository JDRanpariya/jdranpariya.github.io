## Overlooked Thinkers on Computation & Emergence

🔹 **Alan Turing** (Beyond the Turing Machine)
- 📖 *The Chemical Basis of Morphogenesis* (1952)
- First theory of **self-organization in biology** (reaction-diffusion systems).
- Inspired modern artificial life and morphogenetic algorithms.

🔹 **John von Neumann** (Beyond Digital Computing)
- 📖 *Theory of Self-Reproducing Automata* (1966, posthumous)
- Introduced the first model of **cellular automata and artificial life**.
- Predicted the possibility of machines that **evolve and learn**.

🔹 **Edward Fredkin** (Digital Physics & Computational Universe)
- 📖 *Finite Nature* (unpublished but discussed in papers)
- Suggested the universe itself might be a **cellular automaton**.

🔹 **Gordon Pask** (Conversation Theory & Self-Adaptive Systems)
- 📖 *An Approach to Cybernetics* (1961)
- Theorized learning as an **interactive process between systems**.

## Deep Thinkers on Information & Thermodynamics

🔹 **Erwin Schrödinger** (Life as a Negentropic Process)
- 📖 *What is Life?* (1944)
- First to describe life as an **entropy-reducing system**.

🔹 **Léon Brillouin** (Negentropy & Information in Physics)
- 📖 *Science and Information Theory* (1956)
- Extended Shannon's information theory to **physical processes**.

🔹 **Rolf Landauer** (Information as a Physical Quantity)
- 📖 *Irreversibility and Heat Generation in the Computing Process* (1961)
- **Landauer's Principle**: erasing information **produces heat** (linking physics to computation).

🔹 **Myron Tribus** (Bayesian Thermodynamics & Decision Theory)
- 📖 *Rational Descriptions, Decisions, and Designs* (1969)
- Applied **Bayesian probability** to physics.

## Alternative Models of Intelligence & Cognition

🔹 **Ross Quillian** (Semantic Memory & Connectionism)
- 📖 *Semantic Memory* (1966)
- Proposed the first **hierarchical model of knowledge**.

🔹 **Warren McCulloch & Walter Pitts** (Neural Logic & Cybernetics)
- 📖 *A Logical Calculus of the Ideas Immanent in Nervous Activity* (1943)
- **Precursor to modern AI** and computational neuroscience.

🔹 **Humberto Maturana & Francisco Varela** (Autopoiesis & Biological Computation)
- 📖 *Autopoiesis and Cognition* (1972)
- Defined living systems as **self-producing, self-organizing machines**.

🔹 **Valentin Turchin** (Metasystem Transitions & Evolution of Intelligence)
- 📖 *The Phenomenon of Science* (1977)
- Connected **biological evolution to information processing**.

## Forgotten Works on Physics & Computation

🔹 **David Bohm** (Hidden Variables & Holonomic Brain Theory)
- 📖 *Wholeness and the Implicate Order* (1980)
- Suggested that **quantum mechanics encodes information non-locally**.

🔹 **Konrad Zuse** (The Universe as a Computer)
- 📖 *Calculating Space* (1969)
- First digital physics model, suggests that **spacetime is a cellular automaton**.

🔹 **Tony Smith** (Hyperdimensional Computing in Physics)
- Explored computational physics in **8-dimensional spaces**.

## Other Notable Thinkers & Concepts

### Pioneers in Computation & Cybernetics

- **Christopher Langton**: explored **artificial life & the edge of chaos**.
- **Heinz von Foerster**: introduced **self-referential systems in cybernetics**.
- **Stafford Beer**: explored **biological cybernetics & self-regulating systems**.
- **Peter Naur**: argued programming is a **cognitive process, not just logic**.

### Radical Thinkers in Physics & Computation

- **Roger Penrose**: explored **quantum consciousness & tiling**.
- **Carlo Rovelli**: proposed **relational quantum mechanics**.
- **John Wheeler**: coined **"it from bit,"** suggesting the universe is fundamentally **informational**.
- **Julian Barbour**: argued that **time itself does not exist**.

### Minds That Explored Emergent Intelligence

- **Michael Levin**: explored **morphogenesis as computation**.
- **Gerald Edelman**: proposed **neural Darwinism**.
- **Freeman Dyson**: suggested **life as a thermodynamic process**.
- **Karl Friston**: developed the **free energy principle** in brain theory.

### Alternative Mathematics & Formalisms

- **George Spencer-Brown**: developed *Laws of Form* (1969).
- **William Rowan Hamilton**: created **quaternions**, later found essential in quantum mechanics.
- **Alexander Grothendieck**: invented **modern category theory**.
- **Charles Hinton**: explored **4D geometry & higher-dimensional cognition**.

### Alternative Physics & Computation Models

- **Louis de Broglie**: developed **pilot-wave theory** as an alternative to standard quantum mechanics.
- **David Deutsch**: explored **quantum computation & the multiverse**.
- **Garrett Lisi**: proposed **E8 theory** for fundamental forces.
