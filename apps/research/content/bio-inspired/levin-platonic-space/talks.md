# Talks

[Guide](/notes/bio-inspired/levin-platonic-space/) · Talks · [Papers](/notes/bio-inspired/levin-platonic-space/papers/) · [Books](/notes/bio-inspired/levin-platonic-space/books/) · [Mathematics](/notes/bio-inspired/levin-platonic-space/mathematics/) · [People and labs](/notes/bio-inspired/levin-platonic-space/people/)

This page lists talks, interviews, podcasts and symposium sessions on what Michael Levin means by Platonic space, patterns and mathematics. Each entry says if Levin speaks, how much of it is on the theme, and what it adds. Some entries are talks by guests or critics about his idea. Where a video has a key passage, a link opens it at that point. The blog section lists his posts on the theme, newest first.

## Start here {#start-here}

1. [Breakthrough Research in Platonic Space](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GP7S3mrBgYE). A fourteen-minute clip from late 2024 where he first states the idea plainly and says how sure he is.
2. [Platonic Space: Brief Argument and Research Agenda](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EdEqgCOSx7E). The shortest complete map of the argument, with each claim graded by how sure he is.
3. [Patterns of Form and Behavior Beyond Emergence](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v5NLwUZQIH8). The opening talk of the symposium gives the full hypothesis, its arguments and its open questions.
4. [Free Lunches: Model Systems for Studying the Agential Gifts from the Platonic Space](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K8BmMU1Tm-I). Shows the research plan: model systems that measure free lunches as competence out minus effort in.
5. [Thoughts as Thinkers: the Biology and Computer Science of Inspiration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P803O6opd3o). A recent short talk that extends the view to inspiration and to the claim that we are the patterns.
6. [Discussion with Michael Levin about Platonic Space](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=woUSZ4RTwPA). He answers the main objections and says where his view has changed, for example on eternal forms.
7. [Michael Levin and Earl Miller: Bioelectricity, Platonic Spaces, Cell Intelligence and Neurobots](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/126-michael-levin-earl-miller-bioelectricity-platonic/id1752486249?i=1000771174671). A neuroscientist pushes back in real time, and you hear how Levin defends the idea as a research programme.
8. [Constraint, Not Ingression: Against Levin's Platonic Biology (Richard Preschel)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_JAQMCkI1Gc). A critic argues that physical constraint explains the same patterns, so you hear the strongest case against.

- [Where Does Intelligence Live? Michael Levin on Evolution, AI, and Platonic Space](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fpIkuRr4MCQ), 632nm, 2026-10 uploaded, interview. Levin says selection as the source of all traits is an axiom and has not been shown. Free lunches are enablements, and a four-node network already does Pavlovian conditioning. He keeps the word Platonic because mathematicians know it. He hypothesizes that the space also holds kinds of minds and that we are patterns. Moments: [Free lunches are enablements, a different thing from constraint](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fpIkuRr4MCQ&t=631s); [Why he keeps the term Platonic space](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fpIkuRr4MCQ&t=985s); [The space may also hold high-agency patterns, or kinds of minds](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fpIkuRr4MCQ&t=1136s).
