# information, compression and learning dynamics

I'm interested in understanding and reverse engineering intelligence to build better robots. Intelligence is very hard to define, but I like Levin's idea of [diverse intelligence](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Or_3tlEOLj4).

Anything you can formalize can be simulated but does substrate only matters for cost? carrying tradeoff for energy, time, parallelism, and noise tolerance or has other effects?

We need our agents to be good enough!

> Finite agents must "satisfice" because true optimization is computationally impossible in the real world.
> — Simon

## Areas of Interest

- **Energy**: Be it expanding outwards on the [Kardashev scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kardashev_scale) or controlling smaller scales on the Barrow scale, to make giant civilizational leaps we need the ability to harness energy in exceedingly large amounts. How can we have abundant energy, and what would it take to move industry away from oil and other finite resources?

- **Art of Formalization**: Most important skill of doing science is to know how to formalize a fantasy, abstract idea or an observation from nature. Does it always have to be symbolic? or can be neural and represented in latent space? how to map pure math efficiently on natural sciences and engineering?

- **Information and Computation**: Can everything in nature be represented and understood in terms of information and computation? including biology and physics. What are the thermodynamic limits of computation and learning? Which biological mechanisms are portable computational principles, and which are consequences of biological constraints? What can we copy, should discard, and where can designed systems do better?

- **Embodied intelligence and control**: What is Intelligence? How should language, reasoning, planning, skills, control, and reflexes work together? What computation should happen in the body itself, and when do compliance, passive dynamics, actuation, and morphology reduce what a controller has to learn? How should vision, touch, proprioception, and action be modeled?

- **Learning and Memory**: How can an agent learn useful skills with far less real-world data without forgetting old skills or growing without bound? What and how an experience should be replayed, compressed, integrated, or forgotten when an agent is offline, and how can errors during physical practice decide what gets consolidated?What should come from demonstration, simulation, exploration, or practice on hardware? Can a agent reorganize, specialize, prune, reuse, and repair its own structure as its experience changes?

_Embodied Intelligence deals with problem of perception, internal representations and control. I'm figuring how to define it well._
