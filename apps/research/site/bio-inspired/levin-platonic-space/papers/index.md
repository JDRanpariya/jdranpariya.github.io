# Papers

Part of the field guide [Michael Levin on Platonic space, patterns and mathematics](/bio-inspired/levin-platonic-space/).

This page lists the papers behind the guide. The core papers state the Platonic space idea. The model-system papers give the simulations and formal tools he uses as evidence, with a note on how strong each one is. A short list of other papers gives background terms. Some key texts are not openly available.

## Core papers on Platonic space {#core-papers-on-platonic-space}

- [Ingressing Minds: Causal, Non-Physical Patterns In-Form Natural, Synthetic, and Hybrid Embodiments](https://doi.org/10.3390/philosophies11050161), Michael Levin, 2026, Philosophies 11(5), article 161, Published journal version, peer reviewed. This is the main paper. It proposes a latent space of patterns that runs from static mathematical truths to kinds of minds. Mind relates to body as mathematics relates to physics, and a test says when to drop the idea. The February 2025 preprint (https://osf.io/5g2xj/) had the same mathematical examples but no explicit dualism, no falsifiability test and no general claim about mind and body.
- [Selves as Perspectives: From Biological Life to Superintelligence and a Bodhisattva Project](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/s56zu_v1), Doctor, Witkowski, Colognese, Ishihara and Levin, 2025, PsyArXiv, Preprint. The authors treat selves as perspectives that can share content across substrates. They adopt the Platonic space for the field of patterns and extend it to questions of AI alignment and compassion.
- [Self-Improvising Memory: A Perspective on Memories as Agential, Dynamically Reinterpreting Cognitive Glue](https://doi.org/10.3390/e26060481), Michael Levin, 2024, Entropy 26(6), 481, Published journal version, peer reviewed. Memories are active patterns that keep reinterpreting themselves, so thoughts and thinkers lie on one continuum. A note in this paper is his first written mention of an active Platonic space.
- [Robustness of the Mind-Body Interface: case studies of unconventional information flow in the multiscale living architecture](https://doi.org/10.31219/osf.io/fqm7r), Kofman and Levin, 2024, OSF Preprints (journal version in press in Mind and Brain), Preprint. Clinical cases with very little brain tissue and synthetic beings raise the question of where kinds of minds come from. The authors suggest that bodies are receivers or pointers into a latent space, but they do not yet say that the space is real.

## Model systems and formal tools {#model-systems-and-formal-tools}

- [Remapping and navigation of an embedding space via error minimization: a fundamental organizational principle of cognition in natural and artificial systems](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plrev.2026.06.009), Hartl, Pio-Lopez, Fields and Levin, 2026, Physics of Life Reviews, Published journal version; an arXiv preprint also exists. Cognition in any substrate builds an embedding space and moves through it by reducing error. One passage links the convergence of AI embedding geometries to the Platonic representation hypothesis. A conceptual review with no new data, so it is weak to moderate support for the theme.
- [Classical sorting algorithms as a model of morphogenesis: self-sorting arrays reveal unexpected competencies in a minimal model of basal intelligence](https://doi.org/10.1177/10597123241269740), Zhang, Goldstein and Levin, 2024, Adaptive Behavior, Published journal version; an arXiv preprint also exists. Distributed versions of simple sorting algorithms sort around broken elements and cluster by algorithm type, which is written nowhere in the code. This is the model system behind his later free-lunch claims. A clear, transparent simulation that shows our intuition about simple code is incomplete, but the authors themselves give an efficiency explanation for the clustering.
- [Diffusion Models are Evolutionary Algorithms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02543), Zhang, Hartl, Hazan and Levin, 2025, ICLR 2025, Preprint of the published conference paper. Diffusion models and evolutionary algorithms share the same mathematics, so evolution can be read as a denoising process. It gives a formal tool for the idea that evolution searches a latent space of solutions. A solid mathematical construction with benchmarks, but it does not test biology and does not bear directly on a Platonic space.
- [Machines all the way up and cognition all the way down: Updating the machine metaphor in biology](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.semcdb.2026.103668), Levin and Watson, 2026, Seminars in Cell and Developmental Biology, Published journal version; an OSF preprint also exists. The authors replace dumb parts with agency by degrees at every level and describe biology as the study of competent patterns in molecular media. A conceptual argument backed by earlier experiments, strong on plasticity data and weak on the step to agential patterns.
- [Mind Everywhere: A Framework for Conceptualizing Goal-Directedness in Biology and Other Domains, Part Two](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13752-025-00524-5), Levin and Resnik, 2026, Biological Theory 21(3), Published journal version, open access. It gives the definitions of agent, goal and intelligence that his Platonic papers assume, and it judges explanations by fecundity and efficiency of control. A conceptual and pragmatist framework with no content on Platonic space, so it supports the theme only as groundwork.
- [Bootstrapping Life-Inspired Machine Intelligence: The Biological Route from Chemistry to Cognition and Creativity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08079), Pezzulo and Levin, 2026, arXiv, Preprint. A review that links his programme to AI. It suggests that agents from different substrates may converge on a shared space of solutions. A conceptual review with no new data, and it marks the convergence point as speculative.
- [Language Game: Talking to Non-Human Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16321), Zhang and Levin, 2026, arXiv, Preprint. A frozen dynamical system, such as a gene network, is put inside a reinforcement learning game so that its responses get meaning through use. The authors read the results through the Platonic representation hypothesis. A modest simulation, because policies trained on the same reward are expected to converge without any Platonic space.
- [What does evolution make? Learning in living lineages and machines](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tig.2025.04.002), Hartl and Levin, 2025, Trends in Genetics 41(6), Published journal version. The genome is read as compressed latent code that the embryo decodes like a generative model. This gives the vocabulary of morphospace as a latent space. A short conceptual commentary that itself lists where the analogy fails.
- [Alignment Is to a Virtual Governor: A Theory of Coordination in Diverse Intelligence](https://doi.org/10.20944/preprints202607.0220.v1), Lyons, Pio-Lopez and Levin, 2026, Preprints.org, Preprint, not peer reviewed. Decentralized systems align to a virtual governor, which is not a physical object but guides the parts. The paper says such governors may sit in a latent space with mathematical truths. A conceptual argument from analogies, and the Platonic reading is one of two options it leaves open.
- [The Causally Emergent Alignment Hypothesis: Causal Emergence Aligns with and Predicts Final Reward in Reinforcement Learning Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06746), Pigozzi and Levin, 2026, arXiv, Preprint. It measures causal emergence in learning agents and finds that it tracks reward. This gives a tool to measure selves as higher-level causal patterns. A large but correlational simulation, and it is only indirect evidence for the theme.
- [Agnosiophobia in a virtual agent: behavioral and dynamical architecture in Lenia](https://doi.org/10.1162/isal.a.1012), Cool, Hartl, Levin and Petti, 2026, Artificial Life conference proceedings, Published conference paper; an arXiv preprint also exists. Patterns in the Lenia cellular automaton avoid regions they cannot sense, a behaviour that nobody designed. It is a model system for patterns as agents. A careful, transparent simulation, but its explanation comes from the dynamics of the rules, not from a separate space.
- [Diverse Intelligence: an essential field for questions of AI and Consciousness](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/st4vk_v1), Michael Levin, 2025, PsyArXiv (commentary for Behavioral and Brain Sciences), Preprint of a journal commentary. A short statement that we do not make consciousness but build interfaces to patterns from a non-physical latent space. It uses the sorting algorithms as support. An opinion commentary with no new evidence.
- [Animism](https://doi.org/10.26522/posthumanismjournal.v4i2.5036), Alexander and Levin, 2025, Posthumanism journal, Vol. 4 No. 2 (short fiction), Published text. A short story about a child who treats talking appliances as persons. It shows how the question of minds in artifacts looks to lay readers. Fiction, so it is no evidence, and its views belong to the characters.

## Other Levin papers on the theme {#other-levin-papers-on-the-theme}

- [Thoughts and thinkers: On the complementarity between objects and processes](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plrev.2025.01.008), Fields and Levin, 2025, Physics of Life Reviews, Published journal version. The technical paper behind his claim that thoughts and thinkers lie on one continuum. His later papers and talks cite it for the agency of patterns.
- [Mind Everywhere: A Framework for Conceptualizing Goal-Directedness in Biology and Other Domains, Part One](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13752-025-00523-6), Levin and Resnik, 2026, Biological Theory, Published journal version. The first part of the framework, with the experimental cases that Part Two builds on.
- [There’s Plenty of Room Right Here: Biological Systems as Evolved, Overloaded, Multi-Scale Machines](https://doi.org/10.3390/biomimetics8010110), Bongard and Levin, 2023, Biomimetics 8(1), 110, Published journal version. It introduces polycomputing, the idea that one physical process can be read as different computations by different observers. He uses it to answer where agency sits.
- [Darwin’s agential materials: evolutionary implications of multiscale competency in developmental biology](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00018-023-04790-z), Michael Levin, 2023, Cellular and Molecular Life Sciences, Published journal version. It sets out the idea of agential materials, parts with their own competencies, which his later work on patterns assumes.

## Not available as a free legal copy {#not-available}

- Ingressing Patterns of Life, 2026. It is published in print only, with no online copy.
- Patterns are alive, and we are living patterns, 2024. The essay is behind a paywall; an earlier version is his SUTI blog post.
- Free Lunch? Low-Cost Intelligence Through Pattern-Guided Exploration, 2025. This IEEE ICDL 2025 paper has no open copy or preprint.
